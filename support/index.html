Support NPCollab

NPCollab is free, ad-free, and will always remain so. Help us cover running costs.

Building and maintaining NPCollab takes time, clinical expertise, and a small amount of money for hosting. We will never show ads or sell data. If this resource has helped your practice, please consider a small contribution.

Running Costs

  • Domain registration (npcollab.com) — approximately $20 AUD per year
  • Shared hosting (cPanel) — approximately $60–$120 AUD per year
  • Analytics — $0–$108 AUD per year
  • Content development — Voluntary NP contributors
