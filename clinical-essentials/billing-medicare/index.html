Billing Medicare as an NP

Understanding MBS item numbers, billing rights, and how to maximise your practice revenue while staying compliant.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

Medicare billing rules change regularly. Always verify current item numbers, fees, and eligibility requirements on the MBS Online website before billing. Consider consulting a healthcare billing specialist or your professional indemnity insurer when establishing a billing practice.

NP Medicare Billing Rights

Endorsed Nurse Practitioners have direct billing rights under Medicare — you do not need a supervising doctor to bill. This is one of the most significant and under-utilised rights of NP endorsement in Australia.

✅

What NPs Can Bill

  • Consultations — attendances, telephone, telehealth
  • Chronic disease management items
  • Mental health treatment plan items
  • Preventive health assessments
  • Pathology and imaging requests (patient rebate)
  • Wound management procedures
  • Pregnancy support and antenatal items
  • Residential aged care items
❌

Key Limitations

  • You must be billing as a Nurse Practitioner — not as an RN
  • Some items require collaborative arrangement documentation
  • Provider number is linked to your practice location
  • Some GP-specific items (e.g. 3GP, 721) are not available to NPs
  • Mental health items require specific training/credentialing for some
  • Bulk billing is optional — you can charge a gap

Getting Your Provider Number

Before you can bill Medicare, you need a Medicare provider number. This is linked to both your registration and your practice location — you need a separate provider number for each physical location where you practise.

  1. Confirm your AHPRA NP endorsement is active
  2. Apply through HPOS (Health Professional Online Services) at servicesaustralia.gov.au
  3. You will need your AHPRA registration number and practice address
  4. Provider numbers are location-specific — apply for each site
  5. Processing typically takes 2–4 weeks
  6. For locum or multiple sites — apply for each separately

Item Description Duration Schedule Fee
82200 Consultation in rooms — level 1 (brief) <6 min ~$19
82205 Consultation in rooms — level 2 (standard) 6–19 min ~$41
82210 Consultation in rooms — level 3 (long) 20–39 min ~$79
82215 Consultation in rooms — level 4 (prolonged) ≥40 min ~$115

⚠️ Schedule fees and item numbers change. Always verify at mbsonline.gov.au before billing.

💻

Telehealth Items

NPs have access to telehealth MBS items for both video and phone consultations. Telehealth items generally mirror in-rooms items but have specific eligibility requirements including patient location and consent documentation.

  • Video consultations — patient must be in a non-metropolitan area OR MMM 2–7 location for some items
  • Phone consultations — available with fewer geographic restrictions
  • Telehealth items require documented patient consent
  • Some items require the patient to have seen the NP in person within the past 12 months
  • Check telehealth eligibility at MBS Online — the rules vary by item
📋

Chronic Disease and Mental Health Items

  • GP Management Plan equivalent (Item 93) — NPs can create and review care plans for patients with chronic conditions
  • Team Care Arrangement equivalent (Item 95) — NPs can coordinate multidisciplinary care
  • Mental Health Treatment Plan (Item 90) — for patients with mental disorders, enables referral to psychological services
  • Health Assessment items — for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander patients, aged care residents, children, and adults over 45
  • Medication Management Review — NPs can participate in and bill medication reviews in some contexts

Practical Billing Tips

💡

Getting Started

  • Use practice management software with Medicare integration — Best Practice, MedicalDirector, or Genie are common
  • Set up ECLIPSE or HPOS for online claiming — faster and reduces errors
  • Keep contemporaneous consultation notes — Medicare audits do occur
  • Document consultation duration accurately — this determines the item level
  • Register with the PBS Authority line for restricted benefit prescribing
⚖️

Compliance

  • Medicare audits can go back 2 years — keep records
  • Inappropriate billing is a serious professional and legal matter
  • If unsure about an item — check MBS Online or call the MBS helpline: 132 150
  • Document your collaborative arrangement — required for some items
  • Notify Medicare if your provider number location changes
